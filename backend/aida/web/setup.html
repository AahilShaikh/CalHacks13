Aida scanner setup

USB webcam required · 1280 × 720 requested · servo neutral 1450 µs

1. Select and calibrate the USB camera

Plug the USB webcam into the Mac. Keep its focus and zoom fixed once calibrated.

Use the printed 10 × 10 ChArUco board with 20 mm squares. Print at actual size. Hold the board in front of the USB camera. Take 12–20 sharp samples, tilting it left/right and up/down, varying distance and position across the image. Keep the full board in view where possible. Flat turntable rotation alone cannot calibrate the lens. These setup photos do not count toward an object's four-view limit.

2. Mount the board and set zero

Mount the board flat on the turntable, leave it empty, and fix the USB camera on its stand. Keep board corners visible around the object at every rotation. The camera must see both the board and the object. Close Arduino Serial Monitor, connect the Mega and ensure the 1450 µs firmware is uploaded.

3. Scan an object and start CAD

Place the object on the board. Leave the USB camera and board mounting fixed. The agent can acquire up to four cardinal views. Automatic scaling needs front plus at least one side view. The mesh is handed to CAD only after its metric fit passes. Agent mode and CAD use your configured Claude account.

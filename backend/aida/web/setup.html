Aida scanner setup

USB webcam required · 1280 × 720 requested · servo neutral 1450 µs

1. Select and calibrate the USB camera

Plug the USB webcam into the computer. Keep its focus and zoom fixed once calibrated.

Use the printed 10 × 10 ChArUco board with 20 mm squares. Print at actual size. Hold the board in front of the USB camera. Take 12–20 sharp samples, tilting it left/right and up/down, varying distance and position across the image. Keep the full board in view where possible. Flat turntable rotation alone cannot calibrate the lens. These setup photos do not count toward an object's four-view limit.

2. Automatic zero

Mount the board flat on the turntable and fix the USB camera on its stand. Keep board corners visible around the object at every rotation. The camera must see both the board and the object. Close Arduino Serial Monitor, connect the Mega and ensure the 1450 µs firmware is uploaded.

The board's current orientation becomes 0° automatically when the server starts. The server commands STOP and observes the board; it does not rotate to a home position. If startup ran before the hardware was ready, retry initialization after mounting the board.

An empty-table reference is optional for diagnostics. Object-only SAM segmentation does not require it. To save one, remove the object first; this does not change zero.

3. Scan into a workspace

Place the object on the board. Keep the camera and board mounting fixed. The scanner captures front, left, back and right, then adds the mesh to your open workspace only after automatic metric fitting succeeds. You can also ask Aida to scan directly in chat.
